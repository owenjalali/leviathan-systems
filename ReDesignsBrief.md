- show technical depth
- list services

**Time leakage = revenue leakage**

Core belief:

> If a business relies on humans to respond, it is bleeding money.

**Front-facing:**

- Wasted time

- SMS

When the site loads, the visitor should feel:

- Recognition
- “This is about me”

**Mechanisms:**

## 5. The Demo Is THE Centerpiece

Front-of-site experience must:
- Visualize missed leads
- Quantify loss
- Show instant capture + booking

If you don’t have logos yet:

- Use systems
- Use specificity

### SECTION 2 — LOSS CALCULATOR (CENTERPIECE)

Must be visible above the fold on desktop and within first scroll on mobile.

**Inputs (4 max):**
1. Leads per week
2. Average job value ($)
3. Response time (minutes or hours)
4. Close rate (%)

**Output (live):**
- Lost leads per week
- Lost revenue per month
- “That’s ~X booked jobs you never got the chance to win.”

**Math model (explainable):**
- <5 min = 0%
- 5–15 = 10%
- 15–60 = 25%
- 1–4h = 40%
- 4–24h = 60%
- >24h = 75%

Lost leads/week = leads × penalty  
Lost revenue/month = lost leads/week × 4.33 × avg job value × close rate

Footnote:
*Based on response-time decay. Adjusted by your close rate.*

Validation:
- All fields required
- Friendly errors
- Numeric keyboards on mobile
